> **Quick answer:** Delete known disposable files first: old downloads, offline video/music, duplicate media, large chat attachments and unused apps. Review backups before removing personal photos or documents.

When storage is completely full, the phone may struggle to install updates, save camera photos or let apps create temporary files.

## Start with Downloads

Downloads are usually easier to judge than system folders. Sort by size and remove files you clearly no longer need.

## Remove offline entertainment

Downloaded movies, playlists and podcast episodes can consume gigabytes. Remove local copies you can stream or download again later.

## Review messaging attachments

Years of forwarded videos and voice notes can become a major storage category. Use the messaging app's own storage manager when available.

## Uninstall unused apps

If you have not used an app for months and do not need its local data, uninstalling it can recover both the app size and some associated files.

Before removing an app that stores important information locally, confirm whether that information is synced or backed up.

## Do not start with random system folders

Deleting folders you do not understand can break apps or remove data you did not realize was important.

Use the phone's storage screen, app-specific tools and clearly understood personal folders first.
