> **Quick answer:** Check that WhatsApp notifications are allowed at the phone level, confirm the conversation is not muted, disable overly aggressive battery restrictions, and make sure WhatsApp can use background data. Restart the phone after changing settings.

When WhatsApp messages only appear after you open the app, the problem is usually a notification, background-data or battery-management setting rather than the messages themselves.

## Check system notification permission

Open your phone's settings, find **Apps → WhatsApp → Notifications** or search Settings for WhatsApp notifications.

Confirm notifications are allowed. Some phones provide separate categories for calls, messages or group notifications, so check more than the master switch.

## Check whether the chat is muted

If notifications fail for only one person or group, open that conversation in WhatsApp and check whether it is muted.

A muted chat can continue receiving messages normally without alerting you.

## Check Do Not Disturb or Focus mode

System-wide focus modes can silence an app even when the app's own notification switch is enabled.

Temporarily turn off Do Not Disturb/Focus and send yourself a test message from another device or account.

## Allow background data

If WhatsApp cannot access the network while running in the background, messages may appear late.

Review WhatsApp's mobile-data settings and make sure it is not restricted by Data Saver unless you intentionally configured an exception.

## Review battery restrictions

Some Android manufacturers aggressively suspend apps to extend battery life.

Open the battery settings for WhatsApp and look for options such as **Restricted**, **Optimized**, **Unrestricted**, **Background activity** or **Auto-launch**. Names vary by device.

Avoid changing unrelated system-wide battery settings first. Adjust WhatsApp specifically and test.

## Restart the phone

After changing notification, network or battery permissions, restart the phone and test again.

## Update WhatsApp and the operating system

If the settings are correct but notifications remain unreliable, install pending WhatsApp and operating-system updates from official sources. Then restart and retest.

## If nothing works

Before reinstalling WhatsApp, make sure your chats are backed up in the way you expect. Reinstallation can create avoidable data loss if you assume a backup exists when it does not.

Test the simpler permission and battery settings first.
