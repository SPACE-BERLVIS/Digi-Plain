> **Quick answer:** DStv Nigeria's current monthly prices are **₦44,500 for Premium**, **₦30,000 for Compact Plus**, **₦19,000 for Compact**, **₦11,000 for Confam**, **₦6,000 for Yanga**, and **₦4,400 for Padi**. These prices were checked on DStv Nigeria's official website on **9 August 2026**.

If you found a different DStv price on another website, check when that page was updated. Package prices change, and older figures can stay visible in search results after DStv has changed them.

## DStv Premium

**Premium costs ₦44,500 per month.**

DStv currently lists more than 140 TV channels and its broadest sports offering on Premium, including 16 SuperSport channels. It is the package to compare when you want the widest sports, movie and entertainment selection.

## DStv Compact Plus

**Compact Plus costs ₦30,000 per month.**

DStv lists 135+ TV channels and highlights Champions League football, Premier League, La Liga, Serie A, NBA, NFL, UFC, local entertainment and documentaries.

For a sports-focused household, Compact Plus is the main alternative to Premium if you want strong football coverage at a lower monthly price.

## DStv Compact

**Compact costs ₦19,000 per month.**

DStv currently lists 125+ TV channels. Its package description includes Premier League, FA Cup and Carabao Cup coverage, as well as movies, local and international entertainment, kids' channels, news and WWE.

## DStv Confam

**Confam costs ₦11,000 per month.**

DStv's current comparison page lists 110+ channels, with family entertainment, movies, series, kids' programming, music, documentaries and selected football competitions.

## DStv Yanga

**Yanga costs ₦6,000 per month.**

DStv currently lists 90+ TV channels, including local and international movies, kids' programmes, news and selected football fixtures and sports highlights.

## DStv Padi

**Padi costs ₦4,400 per month.**

Padi is currently the cheapest main DStv package in Nigeria. DStv lists 45+ TV channels, including Nigerian free-to-air programming, kids' shows, movies, music, sports news and local and international news.

## Which DStv package is cheapest?

**Padi is currently the cheapest at ₦4,400 per month.** Yanga follows at ₦6,000, then Confam at ₦11,000.

Before downgrading, check the channel list for the package you want. Paying less only helps if the channels your household watches are still included.

## Which DStv package is best for football?

That depends on the competitions you want.

DStv currently positions Premium as its complete sports package. Compact Plus highlights Champions League and major international leagues. Compact includes Premier League, FA Cup and Carabao Cup coverage. Lower packages have more limited sports selections.

Check the exact competition and channel on DStv's current package page before subscribing rather than relying on an old channel list.

## Why do some websites show different DStv prices?

Older prices can remain indexed after DStv changes its subscriptions.

One 2026 search result, for example, still shows **₦22,400 for Compact Plus**, **₦13,500 for Compact**, **₦9,300 for Confam** and **₦5,100 for Yanga**. DStv Nigeria's own current pages instead show **₦30,000**, **₦19,000**, **₦11,000** and **₦6,000** for those packages respectively.

That freshness gap is why this guide includes a verified date. For a price that can change, DStv's official site should be your final reference.

## Are annual DStv subscriptions available?

Yes, DStv currently shows annual payment options on several package pages, including Padi, Yanga, Compact and Compact Plus. The pages describe the annual option as paying for 11 months and getting one month free.

Annual totals and promotions can change, so check the official package page before making a yearly payment.

## How to confirm your DStv price before renewing

Before renewing or changing your package:

1. Open the official DStv Nigeria website or MyDStv app.
2. Sign in to the account linked to your decoder if needed.
3. Check your current package.
4. Compare it with the package you want.
5. Review the current channel list if you are upgrading or downgrading.
6. Confirm the price shown by DStv before completing payment.

If your account shows a different total, check whether an add-on or another account charge is included.
