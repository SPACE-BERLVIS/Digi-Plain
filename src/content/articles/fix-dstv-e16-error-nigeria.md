> **Quick answer:** If your DStv decoder shows **E16**, first make sure your subscription is fully paid and keep the decoder switched on. Then use DStv Self Service or the MyDStv app to open **Manage Account → Clear Error Codes → E16 → Clear Error**. DStv says you may need to wait about **15 minutes** for the error to clear.

An E16 message usually means the decoder cannot currently open the channel or service you are trying to watch. It can happen after a payment, when a subscription is inactive or underpaid, when the channel is not included in your package, or when the decoder needs attention.

Do not immediately reset the decoder or call an installer. Start with the checks below.

## What does DStv E16 mean?

DStv describes **E16** as a service-scrambled error.

According to DStv's current Nigeria help information, E16 can appear when:

- your subscription has not been paid;
- the amount paid is less than what is required for the subscription;
- you are trying to watch a channel that is not included in your package;
- the decoder software is outdated; or
- there is an installation or signal problem.

It can also appear shortly after payment if the decoder was switched off while the payment was being processed.

## First: confirm that your DStv subscription is paid

Before trying to clear E16, confirm that your account does not still have an amount due.

You can check your balance through DStv's self-service channels or the MyDStv app.

If the account is underpaid, clearing the error will not solve the underlying problem. Pay the remaining amount first.

[DStv packages and prices in Nigeria 2026](/nigeria/dstv-packages-prices-nigeria/)

Keep in mind that add-ons or account adjustments can make the amount due different from the basic package price.

## How to clear DStv E16 on the website

DStv's current self-service instructions say to:

1. Keep your DStv decoder **powered on**.
2. Open DStv Self Service and sign in to your account.
3. Go to **Manage Account**.
4. Choose **Clear Error Codes**.
5. Select **E16**.
6. Choose **Clear Error**.
7. Leave the decoder switched on while the request is processed.

DStv says customers may be asked to wait approximately **15 minutes** for the error to clear.

If the picture returns, you do not need to do anything else.

## How to clear E16 with the MyDStv app

You can also clear supported decoder errors from the MyDStv app.

DStv's current app help says error clearing is available from the account-management area. Depending on the version of the app you have, you may see it under **Quick Actions** or **Manage Account**.

The basic process is:

1. Open the official **MyDStv** app.
2. Sign in to the account linked to the decoder.
3. Find **Clear Error** or **Clear Error Codes**.
4. Select the affected decoder if you have more than one.
5. Choose **E16**.
6. Submit the request.
7. Keep the decoder on while DStv refreshes the service.

Do not download a random APK just to fix an E16 error. Use the official MyDStv app from your phone's app store.

## Can you clear DStv E16 through WhatsApp?

DStv lists WhatsApp among its self-service channels for account management and error troubleshooting.

Its current E16 WhatsApp instructions say the customer should verify their account details, choose the option to **Fix Errors on Decoder**, and then follow the self-service flow provided.

The exact menu wording can change, so follow the current prompts shown by DStv instead of relying on an old numbered WhatsApp menu copied from another website.

## E16 appeared after you paid: what should you do?

If you have just renewed your subscription and E16 is still showing:

1. Confirm that the payment was successful.
2. Confirm that the **full amount due** was paid.
3. Switch the decoder on and leave it on.
4. Make sure it is tuned to a channel included in your package.
5. Use DStv Self Service or the MyDStv app to clear E16.
6. Allow up to about 15 minutes for the refresh to complete.

If you paid while the decoder was off, DStv specifically recommends keeping it powered on while the account refresh is being completed.

## Check whether the channel is actually in your package

An E16 message does not always mean your payment failed.

DStv says the error can appear when you try to open a channel that is not included in your current subscription.

Try another channel that you know belongs to your package.

If other subscribed channels work normally, the problem may simply be that the affected channel is outside your package rather than a decoder fault.

Package channel line-ups can change, so confirm the current package before upgrading just to solve one channel problem.

## Check your decoder software

DStv also lists outdated decoder software as a possible cause of E16.

Its support instructions say you can check the software status by using the decoder remote:

1. Press **Help**.
2. Open **General Information**.
3. Select **Information Central**.
4. Check the decoder software status.

If the decoder shows that an update is required, follow the on-screen instructions and allow it to finish.

Do not unplug the decoder while a software update is in progress unless DStv specifically instructs you to do so.

## Check signal strength if E16 refuses to clear

DStv notes that installation or signal problems can also contribute to E16.

To inspect signal information on supported decoders:

1. Press **Help** on the remote.
2. Go to **General Information**.
3. Open **Tuner Status**.
4. Check the signal information shown for the tuners.

If the signal is unusually low or unstable, repeatedly clearing E16 may not fix the real issue.

In that situation, weather, dish alignment, cabling or another installation problem may need to be checked.

## What if E16 still does not clear?

If the account is fully paid, the channel belongs to your package, the decoder has been left on, and the self-service refresh still does not restore viewing, work through this order:

1. Wait for the self-service refresh to finish.
2. Try a different subscribed channel.
3. Confirm your decoder software is current.
4. Check signal strength and quality.
5. Restart the decoder normally if necessary.
6. Use DStv's current support channels if the error remains.

Avoid factory-resetting equipment or changing dish settings unless you know the problem is installation-related.

## Common DStv errors are not all the same

DStv maintains a current error-code directory, and different codes point to different problems.

For example:

- **E16** — service is currently scrambled;
- **E17** — no active subscription;
- **E19** — subscription expired;
- **E30** — checking subscription status;
- **E48** — searching for signal.

So if the code on your television is not E16, use the instructions for the code that is actually displayed instead of applying the same fix to every decoder problem.

## How to reduce the chance of E16 after renewal

The simplest precautions are:

- pay the full amount due for your subscription;
- keep the decoder switched on when making or completing a renewal payment;
- make sure you are watching a channel included in your package; and
- allow account updates to finish before repeatedly restarting the decoder.

A successful bank debit or payment receipt is useful evidence, but the decoder still needs the subscription update to reflect correctly on the DStv account.
