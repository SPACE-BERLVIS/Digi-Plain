> **Quick answer:** Confirm your internet connection works outside WhatsApp, check that the phone has free storage, verify date and time are correct, and review WhatsApp's data and photo permissions. Restart the app and phone before reinstalling anything.

If text messages arrive but pictures refuse to download, the problem is often storage, permissions or a connection that is too unstable for media files.

## Test the internet outside WhatsApp

Open a browser and load a new webpage. If it does not load, fix the Wi-Fi or mobile-data connection first.

Text can sometimes appear to work on a weak connection while larger images fail repeatedly.

## Check free storage

A nearly full phone may not have enough space to save incoming media.

Open your storage settings and free some space if necessary. Start with Downloads, large videos and offline media rather than deleting important photos at random.

## Check automatic date and time

Incorrect device time can interfere with secure connections.

Set date and time to automatic/network-provided where available, then restart WhatsApp and try the download again.

## Check app permissions

Open your phone's app settings for WhatsApp and review photo/media/file permissions. Permission names differ across Android and iOS versions.

Grant only the access required for the feature you are trying to use.

## Check mobile-data restrictions

If pictures download on Wi-Fi but not on mobile data, review Data Saver and WhatsApp's per-app mobile-data settings.

Also check WhatsApp's own media auto-download preferences if the issue is only that images are not downloading automatically.

## Restart before reinstalling

Close WhatsApp, restart the phone and test again.

Reinstalling should not be the first troubleshooting step because it can introduce backup and account-restoration problems. If you eventually reinstall, confirm your chat backup first.
