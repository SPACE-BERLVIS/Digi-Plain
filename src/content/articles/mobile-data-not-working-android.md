> Quick answer: If mobile data is turned on but your Android phone still cannot connect to the internet, first turn airplane mode on for about 10 seconds and turn it off again. Then confirm that the correct SIM is selected for mobile data, check that you still have an active data bundle, and restart your phone.

Seeing network bars on your phone does not always mean your internet connection is working.

Your phone may be connected to the mobile network while mobile data itself is unavailable, incorrectly configured or temporarily stuck.

The good news is that you usually do not need to reset your entire phone.

Work through these fixes in order.

## 1. Turn mobile data off and back on

Start with the simplest fix.

Open the quick settings panel on your Android phone and turn Mobile data off.

Wait about 10 seconds.

Turn it back on.

Then open a simple website or refresh the app that was not connecting.

Sometimes the mobile-data connection simply needs to reconnect to the network.

## 2. Turn airplane mode on briefly

If switching mobile data off and on does not work:

1. Turn on Airplane mode.
2. Wait about 10 to 20 seconds.
3. Turn Airplane mode off.
4. Wait for your network signal to return.
5. Turn mobile data on again.

This forces the phone to reconnect to the mobile network.

It is one of the fastest things to try when your phone shows signal but refuses to browse.

## 3. Check that you actually have data

A phone can display 4G or 5G even when your data bundle is finished.

Check your network balance before changing complicated settings.

For MTN Nigeria, see:

[How to check your MTN data balance](/internet/check-mtn-data-balance/)

For Airtel Nigeria, see:

[How to check your Airtel data balance](/internet/check-airtel-data-balance/)

If your balance is zero or your bundle has expired, buying another bundle may be all you need to do.

## 4. Make sure the correct SIM is being used for mobile data

This is especially important on dual-SIM Android phones.

You may have data on one SIM while your phone is trying to browse with the other.

Open your phone's settings and look for a section such as:

- SIM cards
- SIM manager
- Mobile network
- Connections
- Network & internet

Then look for Mobile data or Preferred SIM for mobile data.

Make sure the SIM with your active data bundle is selected.

The exact wording depends on your phone manufacturer.

Samsung, Tecno, Infinix, Xiaomi and other Android brands organize these settings differently, but the idea is the same.

## 5. Restart your phone

A restart sounds obvious, but it clears temporary network problems surprisingly often.

Restart the phone normally.

When it comes back on:

1. Wait for the network signal to appear.
2. Turn mobile data on.
3. Test your browser.

Do this before changing APN settings or performing a network reset.

## 6. Check whether the problem affects every app

Try opening more than one service.

For example:

- open a website in Chrome;
- send a WhatsApp message;
- refresh another app.

If only one app is failing while everything else works, your mobile data connection may not be the problem.

The affected app may be:

- temporarily unavailable;
- restricted from using mobile data;
- stuck in the background;
- experiencing its own server problem.

If every app fails, continue troubleshooting the connection itself.
